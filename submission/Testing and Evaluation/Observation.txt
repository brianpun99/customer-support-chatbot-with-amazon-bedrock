Evaluation Observation

I tested the flow with 30 prompts covering bug reports, platform questions, and unrelated requests.
The evaluation score was 0.82, which shows the classifier and routing logic worked correctly in most cases.
Most bug-report prompts were routed properly, and platform FAQ questions were usually answered from the embedded FAQ.
Some edge cases were less consistent, especially when prompts were ambiguous or overlapped between categories.
Based on these results, the flow behavior is generally reliable, but prompt wording and routing rules could be refined further to improve consistency.
